Follow a Letterboxd member's public activity in your Tapestry timeline: diary entries, ratings, and reviews, with film posters.

Enter the member's username (e.g. `dave`, `@dave`, or their profile URL). You can optionally limit the feed to diary entries and reviews, or reviews only.

This connector uses Letterboxd's public per-member RSS feed and does not require authentication. Only public activity is available.
