Follow a MyAnimeList member's public list updates in your Tapestry timeline: episodes watched, chapters read, completions, and plans, with cover art fetched from the Jikan API and cached locally.

Enter the member's username (e.g. `Xinil`, `@Xinil`, or their profile URL) and choose whether to show anime updates, manga updates, or both.

This connector uses MyAnimeList's public per-member RSS feeds and does not require authentication. The member's list must be public.
